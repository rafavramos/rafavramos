## About Me

I'm a software engineer passionate about Artificial Intelligence, intelligent automation, and building technology-driven products that solve real-world problems.

My work combines software engineering, AI engineering, and entrepreneurship. I focus on designing and developing AI-powered applications, intelligent agents, LLM integrations, and scalable backend systems that transform complex workflows into practical solutions.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of AI and software architecture, including agentic systems, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), vector databases, AI gateways, tool calling, and multi-step automated workflows.

With a strong full-stack background, I can take ideas from concept to implementation, connecting AI models, APIs, databases, backend services, and user interfaces into cohesive products.

Beyond engineering, I'm an entrepreneur who enjoys identifying opportunities, validating ideas, and building digital businesses. I'm always exploring emerging technologies and looking for new ways to turn innovation into useful, scalable products.

**My mission is to engineer intelligent solutions that combine AI, automation, and great software to create measurable value.**
